F. Think (2 × 4 = 8)

  1. Why did Mendel choose the garden pea? Give two reasons.
    ✔ Self-pollinating so pure lines are easy; easy to cross by hand; short life cycle; clear contrasting traits.
  2. A purple pea plant crossed with a white one gives 50% purple and 50% white offspring. What is the purple parent’s genotype? Show the cross.
    ✔ Pp. Pp × pp → 1 Pp (purple) : 1 pp (white).
  3. Why is a small population of tigers a worry from the point of view of genetics?
    ✔ Little variation; one disease or change could wipe them all out.
  4. A trait is in 10% of an asexual population and another in 60%. Which arose earlier, and why?
    ✔ The 60% trait — it has had more generations to spread by division.

D. True or false? (1 × 5 = 5)

  1. A child who inherits a Y chromosome from the father is a girl. ______ False — XY is a boy.
  2. The mother decides the sex of a human child. ______ False — the father’s sperm does.
  3. Round-green seeds in F₂ of RRYY × rryy are recombinants. ______ True
  4. In some reptiles, egg temperature decides the sex. ______ True
  5. Each gamete carries two copies of every gene. ______ False — one copy.

E. Show your working (3 × 3 = 9)

  1. Draw a flow chart to show how the sex of a child is determined in humans.
    ✔ Father 44+XY → sperm 22+X or 22+Y; mother 44+XX → eggs 22+X. 22+X + 22+X → 44+XX girl; 22+Y + 22+X → 44+XY boy; 50 : 50.
  2. Tall plants with violet flowers × short plants with white flowers → all violet, about half short. Find the tall parent’s genotype.
    ✔ TtWW — half short needs t; all violet needs WW.
  3. Explain how a gene makes a plant tall.
    ✔ The gene codes for an enzyme; an efficient enzyme makes more growth hormone; more hormone → tall plant.

F. Case study (2 × 4 = 8)

A farmer pollinates a round-seeded pea plant with pollen from a wrinkled-seeded plant. The pod has only round seeds.

  1. Which allele is dominant?
    ✔ Round (R).
  2. What is the likely genotype of the round parent?
    ✔ RR — no wrinkled seeds appeared.
  3. Which cross gives round and wrinkled seeds in equal numbers?
    ✔ Rr × rr.
  4. Rr × Rr gives 1000 plants. How many have wrinkled seeds?
    ✔ 250 (1/4).
