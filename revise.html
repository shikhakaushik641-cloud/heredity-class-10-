Variation Tutorial 1
  • Heredity: Passing traits from parents to offspring.
  • DNA copying errors: Small changes every generation add up.
  • Sexual vs asexual: Sexual reproduction makes far more variety.
  • Inherited vs acquired: Only germ-cell DNA changes are passed on.
Mendel's peas Tutorial 2
  • Why peas?: Self-pollinating, easy to cross, clear contrasting traits.
  • 7 trait pairs: Tall/dwarf, violet/white, round/wrinkled, yellow/green …
  • Genetic terms: Allele, genotype, phenotype, homo-/heterozygous.
  • Law of segregation: Each gamete gets one allele of each pair.
Monohybrid cross Tutorial 2
  • TT × tt: F₁ all tall (Tt).
  • F₂ ratios: 3 : 1 phenotype; 1 : 2 : 1 genotype.
  • Test cross: Unknown × recessive: all dominant → TT; 1 : 1 → Tt.
Dihybrid cross Tutorial 3
  • RRYY × rryy: F₁ all round yellow (RrYy).
  • 9 : 3 : 3 : 1: Round-green and wrinkled-yellow are new combinations.
  • Independent assortment: Genes for different traits sort separately.
  • Gametes = 2ⁿ: RrYy makes RY, Ry, rY, ry.
Traits & sex Tutorial 4

  • Two copies: One from each parent; one in each gamete.

Variation and Heredity

Ch 4 · Class X Biology

Heredity = passing traits from parents → offspring · Variation = differences · Genetics = study of both (Bateson, 1905)

  • DNA copying is never perfect → small changes each generation → they accumulate
  • Asexual: few variations · Sexual: many (DNA of 2 parents)

Why variation helps

survive changes 🌡 · resist disease 🦠 · species survival 🐯 (small tiger population = little variety = risk)

InheritedAcquired
in germ-cell DNA → passed onbody cells only → NOT passed on
eye colour, earlobesscar, muscles, a skill

The Dihybrid Cross

Ch 4 · Class X Biology

RRYY (round yellow) × rryy (wrinkled green) → F₁ RrYy (round yellow)

F₂ = 9 RY : 3 Ry : 3 rY : 1 ry · 16 boxes · 9 genotypes

New combinations (recombinants): round-green, wrinkled-yellow → genes sort independently

Law 3: Independent assortment · gametes = 2ⁿ (n = heterozygous pairs) · AaBbCc → 8

⭐ 560 seeds → 315 : 105 : 105 : 35

Tutorial 4

Traits Expressed & Sex Determination

Ch 4 · Class X Biology

gene (DNA) → enzyme (protein) → hormone → tall / short plant

2 copies of each gene (1 from each parent) · gametes by meiosis carry 1 copy · fertilisation restores the pair

Humans: 23 pairs = 22 pairs autosomes + 1 pair sex chromosomes · ♀ XX · ♂ XY

⚠ Sex is decided by the father; every birth = 50 : 50. Some reptiles: egg temperature decides sex.

Beyond syllabus: snapdragon pink (incomplete dominance) · ABO: IA IB codominant, 3 alleles · birds ZW female
