The Dihybrid Cross

Two traits at once — and the famous 9 : 3 : 3 : 1.

🎯 By the end of this tutorial you will be able to

  • Work out the F1 and F2 of a dihybrid cross
  • Explain the 9 : 3 : 3 : 1 ratio and identify recombinants
  • State the law of independent assortment
  • Find the number and types of gametes from a genotype

Following Two Traits at Once

In a dihybrid cross, Mendel followed two pairs of contrasting traits together. He wanted to know: does seed colour travel together with seed shape, or are they inherited separately?

P: Round yellow (RRYY) × Wrinkled green (rryy)
Gametes: RY × ry → F1: all Round yellow (RrYy)

Round (R) is dominant over wrinkled (r), and yellow (Y) is dominant over green (y). Every F1 seed was round and yellow.

The F2 Generation

An RrYy plant makes four kinds of gametes in equal numbers: RY, Ry, rY, ry. Selfing F1 (RrYy × RrYy) therefore needs a 4 × 4 = 16-box Punnett square.

PhenotypeGenotypesBoxes out of 16
Round, yellowR_Y_ (RRYY, RRYy, RrYY, RrYy)9
Round, greenR_yy (RRyy, Rryy)3
Wrinkled, yellowrrY_ (rrYY, rrYy)3
Wrinkled, greenrryy1

So the F2 phenotypic ratio is 9 : 3 : 3 : 1. There are 9 different genotypes in all.

💡 New combinations

Round-green and wrinkled-yellow seeds were in neither grandparent. These recombinants could only appear if the shape and colour genes were passed on separately.

⚠ Book check

In the UDAAN 16-box figure, some boxes are labelled wrongly — e.g. YYRr is marked “yellow, wrinkled” but it is yellow, round (one R is enough). Also, the NCERT Corner answer lists the 9 round-yellow plants as “RRYY” — they are R_Y_, a mix of four genotypes. Use the table above.

Each trait on its own still follows 3 : 1 — round : wrinkled = 12 : 4 = 3 : 1, and yellow : green = 12 : 4 = 3 : 1.

Law of Independent Assortment

◆ 3. Law of Independent Assortment

When two pairs of traits are inherited together, the alleles of one pair separate into gametes independently of the alleles of the other pair.

✍ Solved Example 1

560 seeds are produced from RrYy × RrYy. How many of each type?

Round yellow = 9/16 × 560 = 315
Round green = 3/16 × 560 = 105 · Wrinkled yellow = 105
Wrinkled green = 1/16 × 560 = 35

✍ Solved Example 2

RRyy (round green) × rrYY (wrinkled yellow) gives RrYy in F1. Which F2 types are new combinations?

Here the parents were round-green and wrinkled-yellow, so the new combinations are round yellow and wrinkled green.

How Many Kinds of Gametes?

Number of gamete types = 2n, where n is the number of heterozygous gene pairs.

RRYY → 2⁰ = 1 type (RY) · RrYy → 2² = 4 types · AaBbCc → 2³ = 8 types

Why Did Mendel Succeed?

  • He used true-breeding (pure) lines as parents.
  • He studied one or two characters at a time, each with two clear forms.
  • He used large samples and analysed them with statistics.
  • He was also lucky: the genes he chose behave independently, so the 9 : 3 : 3 : 1 ratio showed up clearly.
✋ Test yourself 3

YYRR × yyrr. What fraction of the F2 has green seeds? (a) 8/16 (b) 9/16 (c) 2/16 (d) 4/16

Reveal the answer

(d) 4/16 — green round (3) + green wrinkled (1) = 4 out of 16.

Helix says: Don't panic at sixteen boxes! Treat each trait separately. Round to wrinkled is three to one, and yellow to green is three to one. Multiply them, and you get nine, three, three, one. Easy peas-y!

Quick Check 3

Q1The F2 phenotypic ratio of a dihybrid cross is:
9 : 3 : 3 : 1 from a 16-box square.
Q2True or False: Mendel’s monohybrid cross gave him the law of independent assortment.
False — independent assortment came from the dihybrid cross.
Q3How many types of gametes does AaBbCc make?
2³ = 8.
Q4In F2 of RRYY × rryy, the number of wrinkled green seeds out of 16 is ?.
